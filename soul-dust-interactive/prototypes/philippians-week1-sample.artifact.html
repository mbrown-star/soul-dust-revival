Simple Daily Prayer

Father, I am dust, and You know it. Thank You for starting a work in me that I could never have started myself. Finish it. Grow my love in knowledge and discernment, make me sincere in the light, and fill me with the fruit that comes through Jesus Christ, so that my life brings You glory and praise. Amen.

Lift Up Your Eyes

Lift up your eyes, man of dust, the work is not yours to finish.

Lift up your eyes, the One who began it holds the end.

Lift up your eyes, the day of Christ is coming, and He will not be late.
